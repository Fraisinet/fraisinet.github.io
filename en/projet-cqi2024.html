About The Competition

CQI Senior

The CQI Senior is an annual robotics competition in which 12 universities each send a team of four students to design and build a robot. The competition combines electronics, mechanics, and programming in a 12-hour challenge.
The competition begins at 8 p.m. on Thursday and ends at 8 a.m. the following morning. During this time, each team must build a robot using the materials provided and then present their approach and methodology to a jury.


My role

As the Director Senior, I was responsible for designing and organizing the challenge. I wrote the competition's rulebook, designed and build the competition field, and developed the electronic and software systems required to operate its different modules.
Building the field required extensive wiring, electronics, and programming to ensure that each module functioned correctly and reliably during the competition. I also coordinated with other team members to create the 3D models used throughout the challenge and help me debug the electronic modules.

Challenge Design

This year's challenge took place aboard a spaceship that had suffered an electrical failure. Teams had to complete five different challenges to earn points:
- Climb a slope
- Play Simon says
- Turn a knob
- Flip a switch
- Move batteries.
Each challenge was designed to test a different aspect of robotic design while fitting into the overall spaceship scenario.
