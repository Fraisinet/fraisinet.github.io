Motor Tester Version 2 (MTV.2)

The MTV.2 is the second version of a portable motor-testing platform designed to provide a simple and versatile way to test different types of motors. The system allows users to control DC motors, stepper motors, and servo motors through an LCD interface and rotary encoder.

The second version focuses on improving the usability, reliability, and versatility of the original design while keeping the system compact and easy to operate.

My Role

I was solely responsible for the development of the project, from the initial circuit design to hardware debugging and firmware development.

I designed the complete electrical circuit, assembled and debugged the prototype board, and developed the C/C++ firmware used to control the motors and user interface.

A major focus of the design was hardware protection and reliability. I incorporated protection circuits on both the inputs and outputs of the board to reduce the risk of damage caused by incorrect connections, user error, or abnormal operating conditions. This allowed the tester to be used more safely without requiring the user to have extensive knowledge of the underlying electronics.
