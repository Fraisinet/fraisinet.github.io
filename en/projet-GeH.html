About The Project

Trivia Machine Repair

This was an overnight hardware repair project to restore a malfunctioning machine used by the Trivia team.
After troubleshooting the system, I identified the microcontroller board as the source of the problem.
With limited time and resources, I designed and soldered a replacement board while reusing as many of the existing components as possible.
The repair successfully restored the machine while minimizing the need for new components.
